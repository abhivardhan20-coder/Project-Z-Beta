# Project Z · Hillview Steps

Open **Play Hillview.cmd** to start the 1 km × 1 km neighborhood. The editable Godot 4.7 project is **project.godot**.

Use **WASD** and the mouse to move and look, **Shift** to sprint, **Space** to jump, **E** to interact, **H** to heal, and **M** for the field map. The left mouse button swings the crowbar once you have found it. **Esc** releases the mouse. While driving, **Space** brakes and **E** exits once the sedan is stopped.

Search the roadside case, collect the medical kit inside Northline Market, and take it to the aid station. The furnished apartment, Juniper Park, and West Ridge Overlook are on the map. Hillview uses a hand-laid street and landmark plan with navigable terrain across its full boundary. Background residential blocks provide exterior scenery.

The physics and interaction checks can be rerun with the Godot console executable.

```text
Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64_console.exe --headless --fixed-fps 60 --path . -- --verify-hillview
```
